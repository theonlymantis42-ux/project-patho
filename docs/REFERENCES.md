# References and dataset links

These are the public sources relevant to the datasets, model components, and reporting methods named in the repository. The links identify background sources; they do not independently verify the contents of any local file or checkpoint.

## Datasets

1. **PatchCamelyon (PCam).** B. S. Veeling, J. Linmans, J. Winkens, T. Cohen, and M. Welling. “Rotation Equivariant CNNs for Digital Pathology.” MICCAI 2018. [Paper (arXiv:1806.03962)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.03962) · [Original PCam repository](https://github.com/basveeling/pcam) · [Dataset record (Zenodo)](https://zenodo.org/records/2546921).
2. **WILDS.** P. W. Koh et al. “WILDS: A Benchmark of In-the-Wild Distribution Shifts.” Proceedings of ICML, 2021, PMLR 139:5637–5664. [Paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/koh21a.html) · [WILDS project and dataset documentation](https://wilds.stanford.edu/) · [WILDS code](https://github.com/p-lambda/wilds).
3. **CAMELYON challenge data.** [Official CAMELYON17 data page](https://camelyon17.grand-challenge.org/Data/). The local analysis described in this repository uses the CAMELYON17-WILDS benchmark/mirror and its recorded revision; do not assume that every CAMELYON17 dataset distribution has identical splits or metadata.

## Model and visualization methods

4. **MobileNetV2.** M. Sandler, A. Howard, M. Zhu, A. Zhmoginov, and L.-C. Chen. “MobileNetV2: Inverted Residuals and Linear Bottlenecks.” CVPR 2018, pp. 4510–4520. [CVF open-access paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_cvpr_2018/html/Sandler_MobileNetV2_Inverted_Residuals_CVPR_2018_paper.html).
5. **Grad-CAM.** R. R. Selvaraju et al. “Grad-CAM: Visual Explanations From Deep Networks via Gradient-Based Localization.” ICCV 2017, pp. 618–626. [CVF open-access paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_iccv_2017/html/Selvaraju_Grad-CAM_Visual_Explanations_ICCV_2017_paper.html) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICCV.2017.74).

## Reporting guidance

6. **CLAIM 2024 Update.** A. S. Tejani et al. “Checklist for Artificial Intelligence in Medical Imaging (CLAIM): 2024 Update.” *Radiology: Artificial Intelligence*, 6(4), e240300. [Article](https://doi.org/10.1148/ryai.240300) · [Full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11304031/).

The CLAIM checklist is a reporting aid, not a certification. Project Patho has not completed every item; the unresolved items are described in [Research status](RESEARCH_STATUS.md).
